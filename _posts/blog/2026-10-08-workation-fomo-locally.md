Let’s add a human touch to my technical changelog.

Workation (a mix of work and vacation) is trending. I understand the appeal: take a laptop, go somewhere nice, maybe to an ocean coast and sandy beach, make nice photos, and call it both work and rest.

Having some FOMO, I tried the local version.

Practical question for people who do workations on the beach: how do you fight the sun? I can barely see the laptop screen outdoors on a sunny day, even at maximum brightness.
